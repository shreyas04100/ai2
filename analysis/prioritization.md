## Prioritization Methodology

Opportunities are scored on four dimensions (1–5 scale):
- **Business Value**: Revenue impact, cost savings, strategic importance
- **Feasibility**: Technical complexity, data availability, implementation timeline
- **Urgency**: Competitive pressure, market window, risk of inaction
- **Data Readiness**: Availability and quality of required data at NovaMart

**Priority Score = (Business Value × 0.35) + (Feasibility × 0.25) + (Urgency × 0.25) + (Data Readiness × 0.15)**

| Model bias in recommendations | Medium | Medium | Regular bias audits; diverse training data; fairness metrics |
| Competitor AI advancement outpacing NovaMart | High | High | Accelerate Phase 1; focus on defensible data moats |
| Regulatory changes (AI regulation) | Low | Medium | Monitor MeitY AI governance framework; build explainable AI |
| Integration complexity with legacy systems | High | Medium | API-first architecture; phased integration; dedicated integration team |
| ROI realization slower than projected | Medium | Medium | Conservative projections; milestone-based investment releases |

## AI Governance Framework

1. **Data Privacy**: Full compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023; explicit consent for personalization data use.
2. **Model Explainability**: SHAP values for all customer-facing AI decisions; human review for high-stakes decisions (fraud blocks, credit).
3. **Bias Monitoring**: Monthly bias audits on recommendation and pricing models; demographic parity checks.
4. **Model Performance Monitoring**: Automated drift detection; model retraining triggers when performance degrades >10%.
5. **AI Ethics Committee**: Cross-functional committee (tech, legal, business) reviewing AI deployments quarterly.
6. **Vendor Risk Management**: Due diligence on all AI vendors; data processing agreements; no training on NovaMart customer data without consent.
